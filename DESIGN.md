# Между волн — design contract

An intimate shortwave receiver in a dim tabletop room. The physical tuning ritual becomes the navigation: a moving illuminated needle, a large circular tuning dial, signal meter, and sound emerging through noise.

Mode: Experience for the receiver, Operate for the host panel. Build directly in code; this is an app surface. Design Taste's marketing direction draw, picture quota and scroll-entrance quota are not applicable to this functioning instrument.

Palette: near-black green room #111716, graphite chassis #303732, warm bone #eee9d8, amber #e9b778, muted sage #a9b6a7. Amber marks tuning and the primary action. Self-hosted Manrope if available, sans-serif fallback.

Composition: restrained masthead, short left-aligned invitation, one dominant full-width receiver. At desktop the radio has a left speaker, central display and right tuning dial. Phone stacks the live display above the dial and removes decorative speaker area. Settings open in a separate host drawer with a scrim and focus containment. Player mode hides preparation controls.

Motion: immediate needle/dial updates on tuning; 180–240ms control and drawer feedback. Waveform represents the live audio analyzer. No entrance that blocks interaction; reduced-motion removes travel.

No external photographs are needed: all geometry is the actual operable receiver UI, not a product illustration or a marketing mockup. Typeface maximum one family. Maintain visible focus, 44px targets, readable labels and keyboard tuning.

Impeccable context and concept engine unavailable in the installed environment; its local references were read directly. No engine result or random seed is claimed.
